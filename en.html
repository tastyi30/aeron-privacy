AERON Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026.

AERON is a home screen (launcher) for Android car displays. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored and when it leaves the device.

1. The essentials

  • AERON has no server of its own. We, the AERON developers, do not receive your data.
  • No advertising and no analytics. Data is not sold or shared for advertising.
  • Almost everything is stored only on your device. Data only leaves the device for services that you turn on or set up, as explained below.
  • You can see and delete your data in Settings → Privacy.

2. Data stored on your device

None of this data leaves the device, except in the cases listed in section 3.

  • Car data: what the OBD adapter reads (speed, RPM, temperatures, fault codes, VIN and control units) and the black-box alerts.
  • Trips and frequent places: distance, duration and learned destinations, used to suggest destinations.
  • Where you parked: the last place you parked, so AERON can tell you and guide you there on foot.
  • Your settings, your screen layout and any records you add (for example, in "Your car").
  • Keys for services you set up (for example, an AI provider). They are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Local diagnostics: crash reports that are never sent anywhere.

3. When data leaves the device

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): only if you agree. Your approximate location (about 1 km accuracy) is sent to api.open-meteo.com every 15 minutes while AERON is open, to show the weather. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • AI assistant: only if you set up your own key or server. The recent conversation is sent to the provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI or your own server). Car data, or what is playing, is sent only if you ask for it. That provider handles the data under its own policy.
  • ElevenLabs voice: only if you set up your key. Each sentence the assistant says is sent as text to ElevenLabs to generate the voice.
  • Speech recognition: what you say is processed by the speech recognition service set up in Android, which may process the audio off the device. AERON only receives the text.
  • Maps: the map piece uses the Google Maps SDK, which is covered by Google's policy.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles them. AERON only learns whether your purchase is active; it never sees your payment details.
  • Other apps: when you ask AERON to navigate, send a message or search, the destination or the text is handed to the app you choose (navigation, messaging, YouTube…). From then on, that app handles it.

4. Data between your devices

  • AERON phone app: if you pair it with the display, the two talk directly over your local Wi-Fi, after pairing with a QR code. The data does not go through any server of ours.
  • Co-pilot mode: AERON serves a page on your local network. Only someone who scans the code shown on the display can open it.
  • AERON Centro (optional): if you install it on your computer, data goes from the display to your computer. It does not go through any server of ours.

5. Permissions AERON asks for, and why

  • Location:
    • weather, trips and where you parked;
    • GPS speed and speed camera alerts;
    • automatic brightness and night mode (sunrise and sunset are calculated on the device).
  • Microphone: voice commands for the assistant.
  • Calendar (read-only): the "Calendar" piece, if you add it to your screen.
  • Music and audio on the device: to play your local music.
  • Notifications: the music player, reminders and assistant alerts.
  • Notification access: to control the music that is playing and to reopen AERON when the car starts. AERON does not store your notifications.
  • Do Not Disturb: only when you ask for it.
  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi: to connect to the OBD adapter, to Bluetooth lights and to your phone. Bluetooth is never used to work out where you are.
  • Modify system settings: to change the screen brightness.
  • Uninstall apps: only when you ask from the app drawer, with Android's confirmation.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on the device until you delete it.
  • In Settings → Privacy you can delete:
    • trips and places;
    • the AI history and memories;
    • the voice cache;
    • diagnostics.
  • Uninstalling AERON deletes all of its data from the device.

7. Children

AERON is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes and contact
